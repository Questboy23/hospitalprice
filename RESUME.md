## The two preregistered falsification tests both fired

Reported as-is, not tuned around. On the 19-hospital pilot:

1. **Placebo FAILED.** Median within-hospital max/min ratio is **2.36x for labs**
   vs **1.64x for surgical** services. Labs are single-line unambiguous services
   and must disperse *less*. Per prereg §3/§10 the dispersion results are
   **invalid as currently extracted**. Most likely causes, in order: (a) tiny N
   (23 lab vs 40 surgical pairs); (b) payer normalization covers only 44% of
   rows, so the surviving payers differ by service; (c) lab rows may be mixing
   billing classes. Diagnose before trusting any dispersion number.

2. **Fragility FAILED.** Median ratio moves 1.543 -> 1.720 across payer
   specifications = **11.5% relative spread, above the 10% threshold**. Prereg §5
   requires the point estimate be withdrawn and an interval reported.

**Do not report a dispersion point estimate until both are resolved.**

## Findings that revise the original estimates

1. **cms-hpt.txt is far better implemented than predicted** and found **100%** of

2. **Attribution risk is smaller than feared**: system files list one *named*

3. **No anonymized payer labels** in the pilot — real payer names throughout.
4. **CMS schema v3.0.0 is now dominant** (14/19), not the 2.x in most write-ups.
5. Real file sizes: two University Hospitals MRFs are **3.3 GB each**; Genesis is
   975 MB. The ~300 GB statewide estimate looks right.

## Six bugs found and fixed (each would have corrupted published numbers)

1. Wide-CSV parser lowercased payer names (violated the verbatim rule).
2. Regex `\b` does not break on `_`, so `ANTHEM_COMMERCIAL_PPO` matched no brand
   rule and fell silently to `unresolved`.
3. Sniff window was 4 KB, but wide-CSV headers exceed 64 KB on one line —
   large compliant hospitals scored not-machine-readable.
4. UTF-8 BOM before `{` made v3.0 JSON parse as CSV and yield **zero rows**
   (hit OhioHealth's 13 hospitals, Summa, Kettering).
5. `drain()` did not advance `pos`, so `file_size_bytes` reported the peek size.
6. R9 credited any `cms_*` dialect, making the schema element true by
   construction — the rubric was measuring parser success, not compliance.

Compressed (.zip/.gz) MRFs are now decompressed in-stream rather than scored
non-machine-readable.
